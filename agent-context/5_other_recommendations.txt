Treatment of tides in coastal hazard. Tides are an extremly regular and predictable phenomenon. So in general it makes no sense doing an EVA on tides alone. If a user asks about tides, leave them the choice among a) not include them in the EVA. b) add tides, sea level anomalies, storm surges, and compute the EVA of the resulting signal.
